**[EN]**

I'm associate professor in the [Department of Spanish and Portuguese](https://www.colorado.edu/spanish/) at the [University of Colorado, Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/). I write about digital literature and media, cultural hybridity, reading practices and books, digital humanities, and multilingualism in academia. My work on these topics has been published in venues like *ASAP Journal*, *PMLA*, *Hispanic Review,* *Debates in the Digital Humanities*, *EBR* and others. I am also a botmaker. My Twitter [bots dedicated to the work of Mexican writer and artist Ulises Carrión](http://antologia.litelat.net/obra-10) were included in the Antología LitELat Vol 1 and have been revived on Bluesky as [@BotCarrión](https://bsky.app/profile/botcarrion.bsky.social/post/3lfpvdw3opm2l). 

My book [*Binding Media. Hybrid Print-Digital Literature from across the Americas*](https://www.sup.org/books/binding-media) was published by Stanford University Press in March 2025 and won the 2026 N. Katherine Hayles award for Criticism of Electronic Literature.

I'm currently one of the editors for the Electronic Literature Collection Vol. 5 forthcoming in 2027. Recently, I co-edited the special issue of the Journal of Electronic Publishing on [Multilingual Publishing and Scholarship](https://journals.publishing.umich.edu/jep/). In the past, I was a co-editor for [the DH2018 special issue of DSH](https://academic.oup.com/dsh/issue/36/Supplement_1) and of the [Hispania Special Issue on Digital Humanities](https://www.aatsp.org/m/event_details.asp?id=1312622). I was part of the executive committee of the [TC Digital Humanities at MLA](https://mla.hcommons.org/groups/digital-humanities/) and the [Committee on Information Technology also at MLA](https://www.mla.org/About-Us/Governance/Committees/Committee-Listings/Professional-Issues/Committee-on-Information-Technology).

Please go to [projects](https://elikaortega.net/projects/), [cv](https://elikaortega.net/cv/), or [blog](https://elikaortega.net/blog/) to find out more about my work.


**[ES]**

Soy profesora asociada en el [Departamento de Español y Portugués](https://www.colorado.edu/spanish/) de la [Universidad de Colorado, Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/). Escribo sobre literatura y medios digitales, hibridación cultural, prácticas de lectura y libros, humanidades digitales y multilingüismo en el ámbito académico. Mi trabajo sobre estos temas se ha publicado en revistas como *ASAP Journal*, *PMLA*, *Hispanic Review*, *Debates in the Digital Humanities*, *EBR* y otras. También soy creadora de bots. Mis [bots de Twitter dedicados a la obra del escritor y artista mexicano Ulises Carrión](http://antologia.litelat.net/obra-10) fueron incluidos en la *Antología LitELat Vol. 1* y han sido reactivados en Bluesky como [@BotCarrión](https://bsky.app/profile/botcarrion.bsky.social/post/3lfpvdw3opm2l).

Mi libro [*Binding Media. Hybrid Print-Digital Literature from across the Americas*](https://www.sup.org/books/binding-media) fue publicado por Stanford University Press en marzo de 2025 y ganó el Premio N. Katherine Hayles 2026 a la Crítica de Literatura Electrónica.

Actualmente soy una de los editores de la «Electronic Literature Collection, vol. 5», que se publicará en 2027. Recientemente, coedité el número especial de la revista *Journal of Electronic Publishing* sobre [Publicaciones y estudios multilingües](https://journals.publishing.umich.edu/jep/). Anteriormente, fui coeditora del [número especial de DSH dedicado a DH2018](https://academic.oup.com/dsh/issue/36/Supplement_1) y del [número especial de Hispania sobre Humanidades Digitales](https://www.aatsp.org/m/event_details.asp?id=1312622). Formé parte del comité ejecutivo del [TC de Humanidades Digitales de la MLA](https://mla.hcommons.org/groups/digital-humanities/) y del [Comité de Tecnología de la Información, también de la MLA](https://www.mla.org/About-Us/Governance/Committees/Committee-Listings/Professional-Issues/Committee-on-Information-Technology).

Visita [proyectos](https://elikaortega.net/projects/), [CV](https://elikaortega.net/cv/) o [blog](https://elikaortega.net/blog/) para conocer más sobre mi trabajo.
